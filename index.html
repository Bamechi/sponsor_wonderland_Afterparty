04 — The partnership philosophy

A strong brand integration should feel useful, relevant and natural to the experience.

A hydration partner should be where people need hydration.

A recovery company should be inside recovery.

A beverage brand should be poured and tasted.

A furniture or design partner should shape how a space feels.

A technology partner should be placed where guests can actually interact with the product.

A hospitality brand should live inside the guest experience.

The goal is to create partnerships guests can experience. That is where the value increases.
